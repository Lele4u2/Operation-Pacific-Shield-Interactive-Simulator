OPS LEARNING ACADEMY

MISSION

The combined force disperses from Main Operating Bases to establish and sustain Forward Operating Sites. The official objective is to accumulate legitimate Mission Points and achieve the instructor-defined victory condition.

MOB AND FOS

MOB = Main Operating Base. FOS = Forward Operating Site. MOB teams assess, prepare and support the FOS.

WHY RFIs MATTER

RFIs tell planners what an airfield can support and which capabilities or equipment may be needed. The initial five should help you make planning decisions.

OFFICIAL MP VALUES IN THE GUIDE

Complete all ten RFIs for an airfield: 5 MP. Qualifying fighter sortie: 5 MP. Instructor-adjudicated target results: 10-strength = 5 MP, 12-strength = 7 MP, and 20-strength = 10 MP.

PLANNING SEQUENCE

  1. Review exercise orders and access limitations.
  2. Select suitable FOS locations.
  3. Request the initial five RFIs.
  4. Identify required commodities, personnel and equipment.
  5. Coordinate aircraft allocation and load planning.
  6. Complete the relevant ATO information.
  7. Execute authorized tasks and respond to events.
  8. Review performance with the GM.

The simplified fuel, cargo and landing rules in this website are teaching aids, not official aircraft movement or ATO adjudication.
