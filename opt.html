MISSION LOG — WHAT HAPPENED AND WHY

OPS LEARNING ACADEMY

WHAT IS THE MISSION?

In the official OPS exercise, CJTF Agile Dragon disperses from Main Operating Bases (MOBs) and establishes and sustains Forward Operating Sites (FOSs). Teams coordinate aircraft, personnel, equipment, logistics and mission operations to earn Mission Points and achieve the instructor-defined victory condition.

WHO DOES WHAT?

CAOC: Coordinates air operations, aircraft allocations and applicable ATO execution.

MOB: Selects FOSs, requests RFIs, plans personnel and equipment and coordinates mobility.

CSpOC: Coordinates space support under the applicable exercise rules.

MEDCOM: Coordinates medical support, patient tracking and applicable medical events.

GM: Provides exercise adjudication and authorized RFI results, and maintains scoring.

TEN AIRFIELD RFIs

Crash Fire Rescue, Mobility Support, Ramp Space, Air Traffic Control, Equipment, Bed Down, Fuel, Airfield Security, Local Community Support, Medical.

Teams select their initial five RFIs for each FOS. Completing all ten airfield RFIs earns 5 official Mission Points in the cited OPS guide.

OTHER OFFICIAL MISSION POINT VALUES

Qualifying fighter sortie from a FOS into an activated area: 5 MP. Target adjudication: 10-strength = 5 MP; 12-strength = 7 MP; 20-strength = 10 MP. Those conditions require the exercise's actual orders, tasks, counters and GM adjudication.

WHAT SHOULD YOU DO FIRST?

  1. Review the EXORD / FRAGORD and political access.
  2. Select suitable FOS locations.
  3. Request the five most useful initial RFIs.
  4. Identify needed personnel, commodities and equipment.
  5. Coordinate mobility aircraft allocations.
  6. Complete relevant load plans and ATO entries.
  7. Execute approved tasks and manage events.
  8. Report progress and prepare for the next turn.

IMPORTANT

The continuous aircraft animation, mock cargo capacity, example RFI answers, training pressure and fictional threat events are teaching aids, not the official board game's adjudication. For a real evaluated exercise, use your authorized guide and Game Master.
