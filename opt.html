Choose your OPS station. The other three stations will be controlled by AI. Review mission requirements, request RFIs, coordinate flights, load cargo, and execute the operation.

This public version uses fictional practice data. It is not official OPS execution software.

HOW TO PLAY

1. Review the mission and political-access assessment.

2. MOB selects five initial RFIs. The simulated GM returns exercise results.

3. MOB requests aircraft. CAOC allocates it. MOB loads cargo and submits the flight plan.

4. CAOC issues clearance/PPR after checking the flight plan.

5. Execute the turn to move cargo and update the airfield.

6. MEDCOM and CSpOC handle their own support tasks.
