OPERATION PACIFIC SHIELD

Choose a command station. Work through a verified high-level OPS sequence: mission and political-access review, initial five RFIs, GM assessment, CAOC allocation, individual aircraft loading, completed ATO, PPR clearance, and execution. AI staffs unoccupied stations.

This independently authored training simulation intentionally does not expose official execution-only scenario data, hidden adjudications, or the original restricted board graphics. Site and aircraft capacities are visibly marked as practice assumptions where the source has not been verified.

HOW OPS WORKS

1. Choose one station and review the mission order and political access. 2. MOB chooses five initial RFIs and the simulated GM provides results. 3. MOB identifies FOS requirements and requests aircraft. 4. CAOC allocates aircraft. 5. MOB loads physical counters and fills the ATO. 6. CAOC checks access, airfield capability and workload before issuing PPR. 7. Execute the turn to move aircraft and cargo; review the audit. 8. Repeat for follow-on RFIs and tasks. CSpOC and MEDCOM contribute on their own workstations.

All role actions, capacities, and adjudications in this public demonstration use fictional teaching data unless explicitly confirmed by the accessible guide. This is not an official certified replica.
