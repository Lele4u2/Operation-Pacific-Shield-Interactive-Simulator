How Operation Pacific Shield Works

Mission: CJTF Agile Dragon disperses from Main Operating Bases (MOBs), establishes and sustains Forward Operating Sites (FOSs), and earns qualifying Mission Points toward the instructor-defined victory condition.

MOB: Kadena, Andersen and Yokota, plus Osan or Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam depending on the game configuration. Kunsan and Misawa appear as geographic references only.

RFIs: The MOB chooses the initial five of ten questions for each FOS. The GM supplies results. A completed set of all ten airfield RFIs yields 5 MP. Picking an option without a validated answer does not complete an RFI.

Other qualifying MP: The guide allows 5 MP for specified fighter sorties and points for adjudicated targets. The game does not award those automatically; they require the official EXORD/FRAGORD, prerequisites and adjudication.

Planning flow: Read orders and political assessments → select FOS → request RFIs → identify personnel/equipment/commodities → coordinate CAOC mobility allocation → load plan and ATO → execute under GM rules → sustain and brief results.

Teams: CAOC coordinates aircraft and ATO execution; MOB prepares sites and logistics; CSpOC contributes space support; MEDCOM coordinates medical readiness; GM applies official results. This prototype uses simplified, fictional computer teammate messages.

Game mechanics: Experience points, achievements, the radar sweep, aircraft motion, pressure alerts, artificial time limits and question responses are practice additions, not official OPS rules.
