OPS — RULES & LEARNING ACADEMY

WHAT IS YOUR MISSION?

Operation Pacific Shield is a joint training wargame. The teams work together to achieve mission objectives, coordinate resources, establish operating locations, and accumulate Mission Points.

COMMAND TEAMS

CAOC: Coordinates airpower, mobility and command decisions. MOB: Plans, establishes and sustains operating locations. CSpOC: Supports space and intelligence coordination. MEDCOM: Focuses on medical readiness and casualty support.

WHAT DO THESE WORDS MEAN?

RFI: Request for Information. Learn about a location before committing resources.

MOB: Main Operating Base. Supports personnel, aircraft and logistics.

FOS: Forward Operating Site. A location established to support mission requirements.

ATO: Air Tasking Order. Used to coordinate air missions.

MP: Mission Points. A measure of progress toward exercise objectives.

DP: Demoralization Points. Reflect adverse personnel and sustainment conditions in the official exercise.

HOW TO PLAY THIS PRACTICE GAME

  1. Select one of the five Pacific bases.
  2. Request an assessment to learn its needs.
  3. Dispatch an airlift carrying fictional supply resources.
  4. Let the aircraft reach its destination.
  5. Establish an operating capability.
  6. Improve protection and personnel sustainment.
  7. Respond to computer-generated events.
  8. Build mission points and review your debrief.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

The official OPS exercise uses instructor adjudication, event and risk cards, aircraft movement constraints, political access assessments, resource counters, and instructor-defined victory conditions. This website teaches those broad concepts using simplified fictional costs, scoring and movement. It is not the official game engine.

HOST / JOIN MULTIPLAYER
