AFEX Training Academy

Learn the basic concepts before entering the mission simulator.

01 // UNDERSTAND

What is AFEX?

The Air Force Employment Exercise introduces airpower planning, mission coordination, and the decisions required to employ forces.

02 // PLAN

Build a Mission

Understand the assigned task, available resources, objectives, and limitations before deciding on a course of action.

03 // PACKAGE

Force Packaging

Combine complementary capabilities, such as reconnaissance, protection, and support, to accomplish the objective.

04 // COORDINATE

Work as a Team

Communicate between roles, confirm responsibilities, and maintain a common understanding of the mission.

05 // EXECUTE

Monitor the Mission

Observe changes, review new information, and adjust your plan when conditions change.

06 // REVIEW

After-Action Review

Evaluate decisions, identify lessons learned, and explain what could be improved.
